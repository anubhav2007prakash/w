# Mission LiFE Integration — MyMausam 2.0

Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for the Environment) is embedded across the full product experience, not limited to a single feature.

## Integration Points

### 1. Energy Optimization Module
- Identifies low-carbon windows for heavy appliance usage.
- Displays real-time renewable energy percentage on the grid.
- Calculates daily carbon savings achievable by shifting loads.

### 2. Solar PV Estimator
- Encourages rooftop solar adoption with concrete ₹ savings projections.
- Quantifies annual CO₂ offset per household.

### 3. Persona Recommendations
All 8 personas include Mission LiFE actions:
- **Health**: Reduce outdoor activity when AQI > 150 → reduces personal carbon footprint.
- **Farmer**: Drip irrigation advisory during low-wind windows → reduces water waste.
- **Commuter**: Suggests cycling or WFH during high pollution + traffic days.
- **EV Driver**: Optimal EV charging windows aligned with solar surplus hours.

### 4. Activity Planner
Outdoor activity scheduling considers weather suitability AND carbon impact of transportation mode.
