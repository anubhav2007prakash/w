# Smart India Hackathon 2024 — Problem Statement

**Project**: MyMausam 2.0
**Team**: AeroCoders
**Problem Statement ID**: SIH-1622
**Category**: Weather Intelligence, Mission LiFE, Climate Adaptation

## Statement Summary
Design a smart weather application that leverages IMD open data, AI-generated forecasts, and citizen crowdsourcing to serve hyper-local meteorological intelligence for 8 distinct citizen personas.

## Problem Statement Goals
1. Serve real-time IMD Doppler Radar, cyclone, and lightning data.
2. Deliver Mission LiFE actionable sustainability recommendations.
3. Provide sector-specific advisories for agriculture, aviation, marine, and health.
4. Enable crowdsourced incident reporting and real-time crowd verification.
5. Integrate solar PV yield prediction and grid energy optimization.
