# Social Impact — MyMausam 2.0

## Direct Beneficiaries
| Persona | Beneficiary Count (India) | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Farmers | 140 million | Reduces crop loss via agromet advisories |
| Fisherfolk / Marine | 4 million | Cyclone and sea state early warnings |
| Urban Citizens | 500 million | AQI and heat health advisories |
| Students & Parents | 250 million | School transport safety alerts |
| Event Planners | 5 million | Data-driven outdoor event scheduling |
| Aviators | 0.5 million | METAR/TAF safety-critical weather |
| Commuters | 300 million | Fog, rain, and route nowcast advisories |
| Solar Adopters | 10 million | Accurate rooftop PV investment decisions |

## UN SDG Alignment
- **SDG 2** (Zero Hunger): Agromet advisories reduce food crop damage.
- **SDG 7** (Affordable & Clean Energy): Solar PV adoption support.
- **SDG 11** (Sustainable Cities): Urban heat, AQI, and flood alerts.
- **SDG 13** (Climate Action): Mission LiFE carbon behaviour change.

## National Policy Alignment
- Supports **National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC)**.
- Aligned with **PM Fasal Bima Yojana** crop insurance digitisation.
- Contributes to **PM Kusum Yojana** solar farm advisory needs.
